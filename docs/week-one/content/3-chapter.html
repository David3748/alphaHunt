The last row taught me the most. The sealed run passed every frozen gate. Then I asked a dumb question — when did the signals fire? — and found half of them landed in March–May 2020, 55 in May alone. Eligibility meant being ≥40% off the 52-week high, and the fastest crash-and-rebound in modern history manufactured qualifying names by the dozen.
