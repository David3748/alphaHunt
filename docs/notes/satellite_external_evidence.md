# Parking-lot satellite signal: external evidence only

**Status: published historical evidence, not locally replicated or verified present-day alpha. This does not satisfy the requested local verification gate.**

Primary source: Katona, Painter, Patatoukas and Zeng, *On the Capital Market Consequences of Big Data: Evidence from Outer Space*, JFQA 60(2), 551-579 (2025), [DOI and article](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022109023001448). Tables 2-3 were checked against rendered PDF pages 561-562; no passages are quoted.

The final sample contains 650 firm-quarters, 44 retailers, 53,647 stores and 3.4 million daily observations during 2011-2017. RS Metrics supplied commercial satellite measurements under an agreement (pages 556-557). The signal is year-over-year same-store parking-capacity utilization growth.

Table 2A(3) predicts **same-store sales, not EPS**: satellite coefficient 0.008, t=4.41, controlling for lagged sales growth, quarter return, characteristics and firm/quarter effects; time-clustered errors. Every specification includes satellite data. Consequently, 69.1%-71.9% adjusted R-squared is not a satellite-ablation gain. No temporal holdout RMSE appears in this table.

Table 3 buys/shorts top/bottom quartiles, using rolling three-month quarter-end cutoffs. Its three-day earnings-window factor-adjusted spread is 4.76% before, 4.73% after stock-loan fees (t=3.90). Raw/market-adjusted after-fee spreads are 4.57%/4.62%.

Audit interpretation: subtracting borrowing fees does not establish profitability after data licenses, execution spreads, impact and processing costs. We did not obtain vendor observations, match historical release files, reconstruct trades or test recent years. No downloadable raw vendor dataset was identified on the article page; a supplementary file is listed. The original sample ends in 2017, so these numbers cannot establish a currently available edge. Treat this as justification for a separately licensed replication, not a substitute for the local negative results.
