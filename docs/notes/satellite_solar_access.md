# Solar irradiance access audit

This audit sought a timely satellite irradiance source for Topaz, California (35.383, -120.067), without credentials or account creation. GOES DSR archive extraction is handled separately. The alternatives below were assessed for access and timing, without fitting another generation model.

| Source | Concrete access result | Timing conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| NSRDB PSM / GOES | Public [dataset query](https://developer.nlr.gov/docs/solar/nsrdb/nsrdb_data_query/) returned Topaz datasets through 2025 using DEMO_KEY. [AWS bucket listing](https://nrel-pds-nsrdb.s3.amazonaws.com/?list-type=2&delimiter=/) is public. | NREL's [2023 product poster](https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy23osti/83375.pdf) describes roughly annual lag. Available historical data do not establish a useful early monthly nowcast. |
| NASA FLASHFlux | [CERES product information](https://ceres.larc.nasa.gov/Data/) documents TISA delivery under seven days; the current ordering tool exposed only recent 2026 months. | A [NASA support response](https://forum.earthdata.nasa.gov/viewtopic.php?t=3579) says older near-real-time granules leave public access after roughly 30–60 days and are replaced by SYN1deg. No workable 2017–2025 original-release archive was retrieved. |
| NASA Earth Observations | Old catalog indexed FLASHFlux solar-insolation maps. Actual archive requests redirected to the [retirement information page](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/nasa-earth-observations-neo/), returning HTML instead of data. | NEO was retired September 1, 2026. Cached catalog descriptions do not supply a usable historical numeric archive. |
| ARM GOES products | [ARM's instrument page](https://armgov.svcs.arm.gov/capabilities/instruments/goes) describes public research data through a free user account. | No account or credentials were available; no authenticated access or account creation attempted. |
| CM SAF SARAH-3 | [Official dataset record](https://wui.cmsaf.eu/safira/action/viewDoiDetails?acronym=SARAH_V003) documents Meteosat-derived radiation and an interim extension with five-day latency. | Its ±65° longitude footprint excludes Topaz. It could support a separately specified European/African target; current pre-2023 historical values are reprocessed. |
| DWD monthly global radiation | A [July 2025 ZIP](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/grids_germany/monthly/radiation_global/grids_germany_monthly_radiation_global_202507.zip) downloaded anonymously, 532,239 bytes. Its internal creation date is August 7, 2025; HTTP modification agrees. | A concrete timely alternative for Germany, but a new independent outcome and release schedule are required. |

The DWD file contains monthly kWh/m² on a 1 km projected grid. Its [official description](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/grids_germany/monthly/radiation_global/DESCRIPTION_gridsgermany_monthly_radiation_global_en.pdf) identifies a blend of station measurements and satellite radiation, covering Germany from 1991 through the latest month. It is not a satellite-only ablation. Recent file timestamps fall about one to six weeks after month-end. These creation dates support availability for those files; they do not certify every historical vintage. Germany's already-public grid generation would be a poor delayed target unless a specific publication gap is established first.

Raw successful access probes are in the task's `work/solar-access/` scratch directory. No useful forecast or trading claim follows from this access audit. The ground-weather comparator and its availability caveat are documented in `results/satellite_validation/goes_solar/ground_weather/README.md`.
