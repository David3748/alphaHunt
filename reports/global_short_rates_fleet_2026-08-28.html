What this book is actually betting

  • Oil-driven and precautionary tightening premiums fade in South Africa and the UK.
  • Mexico holds rather than validates the upward path embedded in two-year swaps.
  • Taiwan finally acknowledges domestic overheating—or at least reprices the probability.
Shared receiver failure mode: renewed energy disruption weakens EM currencies, lifts inflation expectations and causes central banks to validate the hiking premium. The Taiwan payer is a partial macro hedge, not a complete one.
