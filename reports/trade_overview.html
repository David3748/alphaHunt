Top Pick BCPC — Balchem: Choline Chloride AD/CVD vs China $181.28 · 35.7x TTM · 1.7% SI

Thesis

Balchem's BCP Ingredients unit is the petitioner in AD/CVD cases (701-TA-798 / 731-TA-1794) against Chinese choline chloride with alleged dumping margins of 73–314%. ITC voted affirmative preliminary injury Aug 10. Commerce CVD prelim lands ~Sept 17 – Oct 2026 — the moment cash deposits begin and Chinese imports choke off. The stock trades at all-time highs on earnings momentum with zero trade-case premium.

Key facts
  • Petition filed Jun 24, 2026 · Commerce initiated Jul 14 (91 FR 45241) · ITC affirmative Aug 10 (91 FR 52349)
  • Broad scope: all choline salts ≥30%, feed- AND human-grade, covers third-country processing of Chinese-origin
  • Choline sits in Animal Nutrition & Health: $231M sales (22% of co), only 8.1% EFO margin vs 36% gross corporate-wide — the margin-recovery torque
  • Illustrative sensitivity: restoring ANH toward corporate margins ≈ $40–60M incremental gross profit ≈ 15–20% EPS uplift
  • Only 3 analysts cover; none mention the case. Q2 was a record beat
What's priced in: Nothing of the trade case. The YTD run is fully explained by earnings beats — no step-change around petition/initiation/ITC-vote dates. Short interest falling. The 35x multiple is the real risk: you're paying up for a quality compounder, and the case is a free option on top. Precedent (GSM silicon metal vs China, 2013): re-rating happened at Commerce prelims, not final orders — final orders were sell-the-news.

~Sept 17 – Oct 2026Commerce CVD prelim — first cash-deposit trigger, the re-rating moment
~Dec 1 – Jan 2027Commerce AD prelim — second trigger

  • Negative Commerce prelim terminates the case (but nothing is priced in, so downside is modest)
  • Third-country backfill escapes duties (though Chinese-origin processing in 3rd countries is in scope)
  • 35.7x P/E / 21.8x EV/EBITDA leaves no cushion for a macro de-rate
  • Commerce date extensions are routine in NME cases
Trade: Core long at ~$181 sized as event-driven overlay. Add on affirmative CVD prelim. Stop: close below 200-DMA (~$166) or negative prelim. Scale out before final orders. Optional: sell ~$160 puts to get paid to wait. 1.7% SI = squeeze fuel on an affirmative prelim.

Pair Trade FSLR / RUN — Section 232 Polysilicon MIP FSLR $214.28 · RUN $9.16

Thesis

Proclamation 11052 (signed Aug 6, published Aug 11, effective Dec 4, 2026): minimum import prices of $21/kg polysilicon and $0.38/W modules + 15% on derivatives, with an anti-stockpiling clause and grandfathering only for pre-Aug 6 fixed-price contracts. First Solar uses zero polysilicon (CdTe) and sells into a price-floored market — the cleanest winner. Sunrun buys c-Si modules with $15.2B debt and 0.09x interest coverage — the purest loser.

What's priced in: The announcement moved everything, then fully round-tripped: FSLR +18.5% to $250 by Aug 7, back to $214 (+1.5% vs pre-announcement) — at 10.2x forward earnings vs 25–35x in prior protected-market eras, the durable MIP benefit is priced at essentially zero. RUN -41% over 52 weeks with six PT cuts post-announcement — but 27% short interest means the incremental Dec 4 shock (module COGS +15–30%) hits an entity with no margin for error. Cheap ≠ safe; the shorts' squeeze point is Dec 4.
Catalysts
  • Aug 24: FSLR class-action lead-plaintiff deadline (overhang clears)
  • Aug 26–27: JKS/CSIQ prints (US backlog pricing color)
  • Sept–Nov: Commerce implementation guidance, first onshoring-plan approvals; watch import front-running and anti-stockpiling designations
  • Dec 4: effective date — first certification math visible
  • Jan–Feb 2027: first post-MIP module ASPs + FSLR Q4 guide = the real re-rating event
Risks
  • Grandfathering + onshoring carve-outs + partner-country "equivalent action" deals can gut the remedy (the market's fade takes this seriously)
  • Commerce can cut MIPs at discretion (clause 1b)
  • Demand destruction from $0.38/W floors hits FSLR volumes too
  • Crowded solar shorts (RUN 27%, CSIQ 29%) → prefer puts over naked shorts
Trade: Long FSLR / short RUN, 1:1 dollar-weighted (RUN via Jan '27 $8 puts given borrow/squeeze risk). Add FSLR on fade toward $200; cover RUN into any Dec 4 squeeze; hold FSLR through Feb 2027 guidance. Satellite: tiny DQ long (trades at 0.04x book, below net cash, US price floor behind global poly) — deep-value call option only. Avoid REC Silicon (rights-issue meat grinder).
