Excess return definition. Each bond is hedged with a same-tenor constant-maturity Treasury. Daily short excess per bond is the credit-index OAS excess: spread P&L = +duration × Δspread (the short profits when spreads widen) plus carry = −spread ÷ 1e4 ÷ 365 (the short pays the spread each day). Basket daily excess is the weighted sum across issuers, compounded over the selected window to give the cumulative line; Figure V attributes the window result arithmetically, so the bar sum approximates but need not equal the compounded total.

Data. Treasury constant-maturity yields (10Y ^TNX, 30Y ^TYX) are real market data, daily closes from Yahoo Finance. Issuer OAS spreads are an estimated model — anchor curves in bond-basket/scripts/build_data.py calibrated to plausible historical levels (2020 COVID spike, 2022 rates repricing, 2025–26 AI-capex widening) plus small seeded daily noise. Replace the anchors with Finra/TRACE or Bloomberg marks for live tracking.

Representative instruments. MSFT 4.500% ’40 · AAPL 3.850% ’43 · GOOGL 1.900% ’51 · AMZN 2.500% ’33 · META 3.850% ’47 · NVDA 3.200% ’35 (enters 2025-04-01). A research tool, not an investment recommendation.
