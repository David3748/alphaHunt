# Forensic dossier: Arbutus Biopharma Corp

model=stealth/ox-alpha calls=7 tokens(p/c)=20610/23104

## Verdict: TROUBLE
**Arbutus is quietly converting itself into a one-shot IP-monetization vehicle: a $230M buyback with undisclosed funding sits atop a shrinking, non-cash, capped-revenue business**

- Restructuring and workforce reduction executed across both Q1 and H1 2026, including severance AND lease-exit costs - the internal R&D organization is being wound down or heavily deprioritized, not grown.
- Royalty revenue from Alnylam is tagged NON-CASH in both periods - reported top line overstates actual cash generation and depends on recurring Level 3 (unobservable-input) fair-value marks.
- The entire royalty stream is contractually chained to a single third-party product (ONPATTRO global net sales) with negotiated minimum AND maximum caps via OMERS - revenue is concentrated, uncontrollable, and hard-capped on upside.
- Two material subsequent events within five weeks of the Q2 filing - license arrangements with Moderna (July 8) and with majority-owned Genevant (July 21) - almost certainly represent monetization of the long-running LNP patent litigation, with economics visible only in the subsequent-events note.
- The $230M Dutch auction tender ($5.00-$5.75) discloses neither the source of funds nor board rationale, and expressly reserves the right not to proceed 'at all' - a headline capital-return signal built on presumptively one-time litigation proceeds, with zero commitment.
- Licensee Qilu Pharmaceutical was paid in Arbutus common stock while simultaneously generating recognized license revenue - a cross-holding that blurs arm's-length dealing between licensor and licensee.
- Governance and dilution friction: ~13.5% of votes withheld against director Gline (vs. ~5-7% for peers) plus a new 16.3M-share (~9.6%) equity plan whose treatment of legacy awards is undisclosed.

Red flags: 8
Trade implication: Treat as a special situation, not a fundamental long: the equity is trading on the anticipated Moderna settlement and tender-offer optics while the operating business contracts and recurring revenue is non-cash and capped. Do not chase the tender headline before the funding source is disclosed; if the Moderna terms show large guaranteed cash, the post-tender residual cash per share sets fair value - if terms are milestone-heavy or the tender is downsized/abandoned, expect the pop to fade. Risk skews to the downside for anyone underwriting the current narrative rather than the liquidation math.

### Verify next
- Exact terms of the July 2026 Moderna and Genevant license/subsequent-event disclosures: upfront cash, milestones, running royalties, and how much flows to Arbutus vs. Genevant minorities.
- Funding source and firm commitment for the $230M tender - pull the SC TO-I when filed and reconcile against cash balance and any Moderna proceeds.
- Dollar amounts and program-level detail of the H1 2026 restructuring note: which pipeline programs were cut, remaining headcount, and implied quarterly burn post-restructuring.
- Composition and sensitivity of Level 3 assets (Alnylam securities mark, Qilu private stake) and whether non-cash royalty recognition will continue.
- Whether any going-concern, strategic-alternatives, or wind-down language appears in MD&A, and true incremental dilution from the 2026 Plan net of superseded 2011/2016 plan capacity.
