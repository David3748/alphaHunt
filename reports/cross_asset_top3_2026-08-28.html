The best trade is NFJ, but the best hunting ground is broader.

NFJ offers the cleanest near-term contract. BSL provides marked credit plus a term date. SERE offers the largest discount, but requires patience and underwriting of physical property sales.

NFJ tender

Virtus Dividend, Interest & Premium Strategy Fund
Market / NAV$15.59 / $16.66
Current discount6.4%
Tender value*$16.49
Accepted-share upside5.8%

The board has approved a cash tender for up to 25% of outstanding shares at 99% of NAV, expected to commence around 1 September. The discount is observable daily and the consideration formula is contractual once definitive documents are filed.

Catalyst

  • Definitive Schedule TO and tender launch expected around 1 September 2026.
  • Activist agreement with Saba supports execution.

What breaks it

  • Heavy oversubscription creates proration.
  • NAV falls before pricing; residual shares re-widen after the tender.

BSL owns predominantly senior secured floating-rate loans whose values are marked regularly. Stable NAV plus nine monthly distributions and closure of the discount at the scheduled term date produces an illustrative 10% holding-period return.

Catalyst

  • Scheduled dissolution on or about 31 May 2027.
  • Portfolio naturally shortens as the term approaches.

What breaks it

  • Shareholders approve another extension; the fund was extended once before.
  • Credit losses or spread widening overwhelm discount accretion; effective leverage is about 32%.

SERE wind-down

Market / est. NAV60.4p / 98.2p
Current discount38.5%

The board recommends a managed wind-down of 14 properties in France, Germany and the Netherlands. At the current price, investors can absorb roughly a 38% reduction from estimated NAV before losing money, although realization costs and time matter greatly.

Catalyst

  • Shareholder vote on 3 September 2026.
  • Asset sales and capital returns over an expected two-to-three-year process.

What breaks it

  • The special resolution needs 75% of votes cast.
  • Property marks prove stale, sales take years, or French tax litigation consumes value.

Selection and sizing

NFJ: 2–3% event position after definitive tender terms appear; do not pay through a roughly 4% discount without revising the proration model.

BSL: 2% income/event position. Require an explicit plan to exit if the board proposes another extension.

SERE: 0.5–1% only, assuming the account can trade London-listed shares and tolerate GBP/EUR exposure. It is an asset-liquidation investment, not short-duration arbitrage.

Adversarial review

GLM 5.3 Flash was used for independent cross-asset idea generation. Its suggestions were then checked against current filings and fund documents. Several apparent term-fund ideas were eliminated because the model supplied stale or incorrect termination assumptions.

SEIT was also excluded from the top three despite a roughly 54% headline discount: its NAV is stale, gearing is approximately 75% of NAV, portfolio companies require follow-on capital, and there is no sufficiently precise distribution timetable.
